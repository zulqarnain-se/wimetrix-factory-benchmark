## Key Metrics at a Glance

- **Shell fabric is the largest single line: 35–42% of sales.** Every 1% cut in fabric consumption (cutting allowances, end-bits, roll-end losses) is worth roughly 0.35–0.42 points of sales.
- **Direct materials total 48–54% of sales**, so material yield outweighs every other lever.
- **Direct labor is 10–15% of sales**, driven by Standard Allowed Minutes (SAM) per style, line balancing, and overtime.
- **Utilities and factory overheads are 18–28% of sales**, the widest band, because indirect supervision (5–12%) rises as capacity utilization falls.
- **Water and Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP) costs are 1.0–2.5% of sales**: a small line, but a compliance requirement for wet processing.
- **Commercial and financial costs are 6–10% of sales**, led by working capital and Letter of Credit (LC) charges.

## 1. Direct Material Costs

**48–54% of sales.** The largest expense category. Small reductions in material wastage produce large improvements in gross margin.

| Cost Element                      | % of Sales |
| :-------------------------------- | :--------: |
| Primary shell fabric              |   35–42%   |
| Trims & accessories               |   7–10%    |
| Pockets, linings & interlinings   |  1.0–2.5%  |
| Packaging materials               |  1.0–2.0%  |
| Processing & chemical inputs      |  0.5–1.5%  |

- **Primary shell fabric:** the single largest expense line. Waste sources are table cutting allowances, end-bit waste, fabric roll-end losses, and shade variances.
- **Trims & accessories:** zippers, metal and plastic buttons, rivets, sewing thread, woven brand labels, care tabs, hangtags, and barcode stickers.
- **Pockets, linings & interlinings:** pocketing fabric, waistbands, pocket flaps, and heat-fused collar and cuff components.
- **Packaging materials:** polybags, export cartons, inner dividers, tissue wrap, and shipment barcode labels.
- **Processing & chemical inputs:** enzymes, softeners, laundry detergents, and dyes used in garment washing and dyeing.

---

## 2. Direct Labor & Production Costs

**10–15% of sales.** Labor cost varies with factory automation, SAM per style, line balancing efficiency, and operator skill ratings.

| Cost Element                         | % of Sales |
| :----------------------------------- | :--------: |
| In-house direct labor                |   8–12%    |
| Overtime & incentive allowances      |  1.0–3.0%  |
| Contract & piece-rate labor          |  0.5–2.0%  |
| Subcontracted & outsourced processes |  0.1–1.0%  |

- **In-house direct labor:** base wages for operators in Cutting, Sewing, Pressing, and inline Quality Control (QC).
- **Overtime & incentive allowances:** premium pay to meet shipment deadlines, cover style-changeover downtime, or recover late production.
- **Contract & piece-rate labor:** seasonal floor labor for manual non-sewing tasks such as thread trimming, hang-tagging, and packing.
- **Subcontracted & outsourced processes:** third-party panel embroidery, screen printing, and specialized washing.

---

## 3. Utility & Factory Overhead Costs

**18–28% of sales.** These costs reflect how efficiently the plant uses energy, water, machines, and supervisory staff.

| Cost Element                      | % of Sales |
| :-------------------------------- | :--------: |
| Factory indirect supervision      |   5–12%    |
| Electrical power                  |    4–7%    |
| Thermal & boiler energy           |    3–6%    |
| Machine maintenance & consumables |    2–4%    |
| Water & effluent treatment (ETP)  |  1.0–2.5%  |

- **Factory indirect supervision:** Industrial Engineering (IE) teams, floor supervisors, line mechanics, and QA management. This share spikes when capacity utilization drops.
- **Electrical power:** sewing lines, central air compressors, industrial washing wheels, and tumble dryers.
- **Thermal & boiler energy:** steam generation fuel (biomass, natural gas, diesel) for washing wheels and steam irons.
- **Machine maintenance & consumables:** needles, belts, machine oil, spare parts, and preventive servicing.
- **Water & effluent treatment (ETP):** water sourcing, softening, and chemical treatment at the ETP for environmental compliance.

---

## 4. Administrative & Operational Overheads

**6–9% of sales.** Fixed expenses that keep the factory operating, certified, and governed.

| Cost Element                  | % of Sales |
| :---------------------------- | :--------: |
| Plant operations & management |  3.5–5.5%  |
| Facility maintenance & rent   |  1.5–3.0%  |
| Testing & compliance          |  0.5–1.0%  |

- **Plant operations & management:** payroll for HR, procurement, finance, IT support, site security, and senior management.
- **Facility maintenance & rent:** asset depreciation, structural repairs, insurance, and land or building lease costs.
- **Testing & compliance:** buyer lab testing (shading, tear strength, chemical safety), third-party ISO, WRAP, and Higg audits, and local environmental certifications.

---

## 5. Commercial, Logistics & Financial Costs

**6–10% of sales.** Costs of supply chain execution, international trade, and working capital financing.

| Cost Element                        | % of Sales |
| :---------------------------------- | :--------: |
| Financial & working capital charges |  2.0–4.0%  |
| Freight & transportation            |  1.5–3.0%  |
| Sales & marketing                   |  1.0–2.0%  |
| Customer claims & discounts         |  0.5–2.0%  |

- **Financial & working capital charges:** short-term debt interest, LC issuance charges, and bank markup fees.
- **Freight & transportation:** inbound fabric and trim freight, factory-to-port container transport, and internal shuttling.
- **Sales & marketing:** buyer agency commissions, sampling costs, trade shows, and brokerage fees.
- **Customer claims & discounts:** On-Time In-Full (OTIF) delay penalties, emergency airfreight, and buyer quality chargebacks.
